# F2 — Plate and Evidence Photos: Design Specification

**Roadmap package:** F2 (`improvement-roadmap.md`)
**Status:** Draft for user review
**Date:** 2026-11-11
**Related:** C2 (bench and tablet mode), E4 (isolates).

## 1. Purpose

Let the analyst take a photo of a plate with the tablet or PC camera when reading it, keep the original unchanged with its hash, time and author, require it where the Section Head says so, and show it to the reviewer beside the result.

## 2. Decisions (confirmed by the user, 2026-11-11)

| # | Decision | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Required | Configurable per test workflow step by the Section Head; optional elsewhere |
| 2 | Where | Count plates (product, EM, water); pathogen steps (selective and confirmatory plates, biochemical / identification); media growth promotion and sterility plates |
| 3 | Timing | Until the step is submitted; afterwards only with a reason, marked "added later". Never edited or deleted; a wrong photo is voided with a reason |

## 3. Out of scope

- Automated colony counting or any reading of the image by software.
- Photos for isolates (E4) and other records; the same mechanism can be extended later.
- Video.
- Image editing (crop, rotate, annotate) — the original is the record.

## 4. Current state (from code, not run)

- No camera capture anywhere in the application. Several screens accept file uploads.
- Pathogen biochemical results have an "attachment id" field that points to nothing: there is no attachment record and no upload for it.
- Readings: count readings, pathogen step results and plate observations, EM / water location results, and media growth promotion / sterility challenge results. Pathogen step results have a submitted time; media evaluations have a completed status; count and location readings have no locked marker of their own.
- File storage with server-side type checks (JPEG, PNG, WEBP among others, by content as well as name) and a 25 MB default limit exists. An image library is already used on the server. PDF reports can embed PNG images.

## 5. Design

### 5.1 Taking a photo

- Next to each reading that accepts photos, a **camera button** opens the device camera (back camera by default on tablets) with a live preview and a capture button; on devices without a camera, a file chooser accepts JPEG, PNG or WEBP.
- The analyst can take several photos per reading, each with an optional short caption (for example "Plate 2, 10⁻¹").
- The photo is uploaded at once. If the connection drops, it stays in the device's unsent queue (C2's device drafts) until sent; it is not part of the record until the server accepts it.

### 5.2 What is stored

- The **original file exactly as received**, with its SHA-256 hash computed by the server, size, type, the uploader, the server receipt time, and, as source context, the device's capture time if the image or browser reports it.
- A smaller display copy and a thumbnail are generated by the server for screens and reports; the original is never altered and is what the hash covers.
- The photo is linked to its reading: test order and step, plate or location where applicable, or the media evaluation challenge.
- Photos are protected like other evidence: never edited, never deleted. A wrong photo is **voided** with a reason, stays visible as voided, and is excluded from the required-photo check.

### 5.3 Required photos

- In Test Master, each workflow step gets "Photo required at reading". For media, the same switch exists per evaluation type (growth promotion, sterility) per laboratory.
- When required, the reading (or the step submission for pathogen steps, or the challenge result for media) is refused without at least one non-voided photo: "A photo of the plate is required for this step."
- The requirement in force when the reading is made applies; changing the switch later does not affect readings already made.

### 5.4 Adding later

- After the reading is recorded or the step submitted, a photo can still be added with a reason; it is marked "added later" with both times, and the reviewer sees the mark.
- After the sample's section is approved, photos cannot be added.

### 5.5 Review and reports

- The review screen shows thumbnails beside each reading; selecting one opens the full image with zoom, caption, author, time and hash, and any "added later" or "voided" mark.
- Reviewers see a count of readings with photos and flag missing optional photos for no one — optional means optional.
- The sample report (not the certificate) can include photos as an appendix, chosen when printing.
- Viewing an original records a download entry like other document downloads.

### 5.6 Permissions

Taking and voiding photos uses the same permission and laboratory scope as recording the reading (and the competency gate, D4). Viewing follows read access to the sample. The step switch is part of Test Master (master-data permission).

## 6. Error handling

| Situation | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Camera permission refused | File chooser offered; message explains how to allow the camera |
| Wrong type or over 25 MB | Refused with the limits |
| Required photo missing | Reading or submission refused with the message in 5.3 |
| Upload interrupted | Kept in the device queue, clearly marked "not yet recorded" |
| Add after approval | Refused |

## 7. Testing

- **Backend `[PostgresFact]`:** upload stores original byte-identical with server hash; display copy and thumbnail generated; link to each reading kind; void with reason keeps the row; update and delete rejected by the database; required-photo refusals for count, pathogen step and media challenge; requirement snapshot at reading time; added-later marking and reason; refused after approval; scope and competency checks; download logged.
- **Frontend (`userEvent`):** camera wrapper mocked at the service boundary; capture, caption, multiple photos; file chooser fallback; offline queue state; review thumbnails and viewer; required-photo message.
- **Manual:** on a 10–11 inch tablet in landscape (C2 target) and a PC with a webcam.

## 8. Delivery stages

1. Photo record, storage, hashing, derivatives, protection.
2. Capture component and links to the three reading kinds.
3. Required switch and checks.
4. Added-later, void, review viewer, report appendix.
5. Documents.
