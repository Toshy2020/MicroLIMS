# Standard Operating Procedures

SOPs governing use of MicroLIMS: sample receipt, result entry, review,
approval, deviation handling, user account management, backup/restore.

## SOP notes from functional packages

These notes are inputs for the SOP authors. They are not approved procedures. Site-specific values are for the laboratory and QA to set.

### Notifications, escalation and working hours (package C5; FS-NTF-012 to FS-NTF-021)

- **Notifications are aids, not records.** A notification, a notification e-mail, the bell count, the "Live updates paused" status and the "record changed" banner help a person to find work. They are not part of the laboratory record, they are not audited, and they are removed after the retention period (`notifications.retentionDays`). The SOP must not rely on a notification or an e-mail as evidence that a task was assigned, seen or done, and must say that the review, approval and result records remain the record. A person who has no e-mail address, or whose e-mail is not delivered, is still bound by the same duties; the Integrations page ("Email delivery") shows failed e-mails and users without an address.
- **Escalation rules and working hours are controlled configuration.** They are changed only by a user with the notification-configuration permission (Section Head or QC Manager by default) for a section the user belongs to. Each change (new rule, replacement, retirement, change of working hours) is recorded in the audit trail. A rule is replaced by retiring it and creating a new one; it is not edited in place. No rule and no working hours are supplied with the system: the laboratory decides the thresholds (in working hours) and the roles, QA approves them, and the SOP should record the approved values and who approved them. An escalation message that says no one holds the role in the section goes to the system administrators; the SOP should say who then acts.
- **Mandatory e-mail types** for a section are a decision of the Section Head; the SOP should record which types are mandatory and why.
- **Live updates** are a convenience. When "Live updates paused" is shown the screens still refresh by themselves, more slowly; the SOP should not depend on instant updates. A "record changed" banner means someone else saved the record: the user decides whether to reload and must check what was entered before saving over it.

### Configuration changes: reasons and retirement (package C7; FS-MDM-038 to FS-MDM-045)

- **Change reasons.** A reason of at least 10 characters is required when a specification, a Test Master test, an item or equipment is edited while open work (open test orders, open samples, incubations or runs in progress) would be affected, and always when a specification is deleted, retired or reinstated, when a test or item is frozen or unfrozen, and when an item is deleted. The reason is stored on the audit entries of that change. The SOP should say what a good reason contains (what changed and why, with a reference to the approving document or change control) and that "see above" or a single word is not one.
- **Retirement instead of deletion.** A specification that a recorded result refers to is retired, not deleted; recorded results are not changed. A retired specification is not used for new samples, and a sample in testing that depended on it cannot receive results until the specification is reinstated or a new one is configured. The SOP should require the Section Head to check the where-used summary (open work and history) before retiring, deleting, freezing or changing a record, to decide what happens to samples in testing, and to record the decision. Reinstating needs a reason.
- **Copying.** "New from existing" and "Copy specifications" create new records that the user must review and confirm; codes, names, SOP numbers, serial numbers, calibration dates and documents are not copied. The SOP should say that a copied specification is a new specification that needs the same review and approval as one entered by hand.
- **Limits stay as configured at the time of entry.** The system does not version a specification or give it an effective date (see CONF-150 and CONF-207). Until that is decided, the SOP should rely on the audit trail and the reason for change to show which limits applied when.

### Bench tablets and unsaved entries (package C2; FS-NTF-027 to FS-NTF-033, FS-AUD-014)

- **A draft is not a record.** An unsaved entry on a bench tablet is kept on that tablet, for the signed-in user only. It is not in MicroLIMS, it is not audited, other users cannot see it, and it is not evidence that a result was entered, reviewed or approved. Only a Save on the open form creates the record. The SOP should say this in those words and should name the saved record and its audit trail as the evidence.
- **Save before leaving the bench.** The analyst saves each reading or form before leaving the tablet, closing the screen, signing out or switching user. The system warns about unsaved entries when a user switches user, but the warning saves nothing. The SOP should say that an entry that was not saved is lost if the tablet's drafts are removed.
- **Retention of drafts.** Drafts older than the retention period are removed from the tablet, and the user is told how many. The retention is the system setting `bench.draftRetentionHours` (1 to 72 hours, 24 by default). The SOP should record the value the laboratory chooses and who approved it.
- **No connection.** While the tablet shows "No connection", entries are not saved to MicroLIMS. The analyst must not treat them as recorded, must not re-create them from memory on another device, and must keep the draft until the save is accepted after the connection returns.
- **What to do with a rejected draft.** A save can be refused because the record was changed by someone else since the form was opened. The draft is then kept and must not be overwritten silently. The analyst reloads the record, compares what is on the screen with the draft, notes the reason the server gave, and enters again only what is still correct, as a new save under their own name. Where the drafts menu shows "Not saved: ...", the analyst also follows the laboratory's procedure for a refused save. The system marks a refused save this way only in some forms, so after any refused save the analyst must check the record on the screen and must not rely on the marking.
- **Discard only when the entry is not needed.** Discarding a draft cannot be undone. The analyst discards a draft only after the same result is saved elsewhere or the laboratory confirms that the entry is not needed.
- **Tablets are laboratory-owned and locked down.** The laboratory owns each bench tablet and keeps it in the laboratory. The SOP should set who may install the application to the home screen, who may change the bench mode setting, that personal accounts are signed out at the end of use, and how a tablet's state is checked at the start of a shift, because the bench mode choice stays on the tablet when a user signs out.
- **Switch user.** A user who has finished uses Switch user, and the system asks first when that user has unsaved entries. The user saves or deliberately discards them before confirming.
- **Late saves.** When a draft is saved more than 5 minutes after it was started, the audit entries of that save show the start time the tablet stated, labelled as not verified against the server clock. The system stores that time as stated and does not correct it. The SOP should say how reviewers treat it, and how a tablet with a wrong clock is reported and corrected under the laboratory's clock check.
- **Updates.** A new version of the application is not offered while a bench form is open, and it is applied only when the user chooses to reload. The SOP should say that the analyst reloads between tasks and never in the middle of an entry.

### In-app help and onboarding (package C6; FS-NTF-034 to FS-NTF-037, FS-MDM-046, FS-MDM-047)

- **In-app help is guidance; approved SOPs govern.** The help panel, the field hints, the first-login tour, the release notes and the list of items that use a test describe the screens. They do not set a limit, a formula, a workflow rule or an approval. Where the help and an approved SOP differ, the approved SOP applies, and the laboratory reports the difference to QA. Every help surface says so.
- **Help is not a training record.** Opening the help or completing the tour is not recorded as training. The SOP should say whether training on the system is required and where it is recorded.
- **Release notes are not change control.** A release note tells users what changed. The laboratory's change control and validation records govern the change. Users check the version shown in About, and state it, when they report a problem.
- **Open SOP.** The "Open SOP" action in a test's workflow opens the SOP for the item from Document Control. When the action says that no SOP number is set, the SOP is not in Document Control, the SOP has no effective revision or the user may not read it, the analyst follows the laboratory's procedure for finding the correct SOP. A missing link is not a reason to skip a test step, and the analyst does not work from an older copy of the SOP.
- **Field hints.** A hint explains what a field means. It does not set the value that is correct for the laboratory. The values come from the approved specifications and SOPs.

### Training, qualification and competency gate (package D4; FS-ADM-016, FS-ADM-017, FS-MIC-015, FS-PHY-017) *(Draft — pending approval)*

- **Competency gate ships OFF.** Every laboratory section starts with its competency gate disabled (`GateEnabled = false`). Before enabling the gate, the Section Head or QC Manager must enter analyst qualifications, review the **Impact Preview Before Enabling** for open assigned test orders, and sign the gate change with a reason and password.
- **Qualification records and requalification intervals.** Qualifications are recorded and withdrawn under `Competency.Manage` with a basis, evidence, qualified-on date, optional attachments, and an electronic signature (self-qualification is refused). Requalification intervals (`RequalificationMonths`) are configured in Test Master (`MasterData.Manage`); the laboratory SOP should define the required qualification basis, evidence standards, and requalification intervals per test.
- **SOP acknowledgement and unresolvable SOPs.** When the gate is enabled, analysts must have acknowledged the current effective SOP revision for the sample's item (or hold an open reading assignment within its grace period after acknowledging the immediately superseded revision). When an item has no SOP number, an unknown or ambiguous SOP number, or no effective revision, recording is allowed and the system logs `SopNotChecked` in the audit reason; the SOP should specify how QA and Document Control resolve missing or ambiguous SOP links.
- **One-time overrides and whole-sample separation.** A competency override (`Competency.Override`) is an exceptional, signed, single-order permission for one analyst on one open test order (never for planned OOS retests). Granting an override bars the granter from reviewing or approving any test on the entire sample, even after the override has ended; the SOP should state who reviews and approves samples when a Section Head grants an override.

### Quality records (package D2) — draft SOP templates

Four SOP templates were drafted from the coded quality-record workflow (FS-QLT, BusinessRules section 15). They are not approved procedures. Square-bracket fields `[SITE: …]` must be completed by the laboratory and approved by QA.

- `SOP-Deviation-Handling.md` — raising, assessment, investigation, laboratory conclusion, QA approval, closure, cancellation and extensions of deviations.
- `SOP-CAPA.md` — creation, plan approval, action completion, verification and the effectiveness check of a CAPA.
- `SOP-Change-Control.md` — proposal, impact assessment, approval or rejection, implementation tasks and closure. The interim rule for master-data changes is a site decision, because the master-data change-control gate is not yet built.
- `SOP-Complaint-Handling.md` — receipt, investigation, laboratory report, conclusion, closure and cancellation of complaints.

Each template lists the conflicts that the site must decide before approval (CONF-228 to CONF-240). A signature in MicroLIMS is the user's identity plus password, with the meaning shown on the signed step. Notifications and e-mails are not evidence that a quality step was done; the signed step is the record.
