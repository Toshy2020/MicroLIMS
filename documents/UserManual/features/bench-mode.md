## 1. What bench mode is

Bench mode is for a shared tablet that is used at the bench. Analysts may use it standing up, sometimes with gloves. Bench mode makes buttons, fields and text larger.

Bench mode is a setting of the tablet. It is not a setting of your user account.

- Buttons, tick boxes, switches and menu items are at least 48 by 48 pixels.
- Text in entry fields is larger.
- Entry windows open full screen.
- The navigation panel starts closed.
- The screen stays on while an entry window is open.

## 2. Turn bench mode on or off

1. In the top bar, find the switch labelled **Bench mode**.
2. Switch it on or off.

The choice stays on this tablet. It is not cleared when a user signs out.

## 3. Number entry and the keypad

In bench mode, an entry window shows a keypad under the fields.

1. Tap a field. Its text is selected, so the next value replaces it.
2. Use the digit keys to type the value.
3. Use the decimal key only in a field that takes decimals.
4. Use the back key to remove one character.
5. Use **Next** to move to the next field.

Keyboard typing also works. If a value is not in the required form, the field shows a message under it.

## 4. Save

- Nothing is saved to MicroLIMS until you press **Save**.
- **Save and next** saves the entry and moves to the next item. It appears only where the screen offers it.
- **Save** is disabled when something is missing. The reason is written under the button.

## 5. Unsaved entries stay on the tablet

While you type, the tablet keeps a copy of your entry. This copy is an **unsaved entry**. An unsaved entry is not a record. It is not in MicroLIMS and it is not audited.

- If the page reloads or the screen is closed, the entry can be restored when you open the form again. You choose whether to restore it. It is not put back on the screen by itself.
- Only you can see your unsaved entries. Another user on the same tablet sees only a count of their own.
- The top bar shows how many unsaved entries you have. Open the menu to go to an entry or to discard it.
- **Discard** removes an unsaved entry. This cannot be undone.
- Unsaved entries older than the time your laboratory sets are removed from the tablet. The default is 24 hours; the range is 1 to 72 hours. The system tells you how many were removed.

## 6. When there is no connection

When the tablet cannot reach MicroLIMS, the screen shows this message:

"No connection. Your entries are not saved to MicroLIMS. They are kept on this tablet until you can save."

- Keep the entry on the screen.
- When the connection returns, the screen says "Connection restored." and tells you how many unsaved entries you have.
- Press **Save** for each entry. The system does not save an entry for you.

## 7. When a save is refused

A save is refused when someone else changed the record while your form was open. Your entry stays on the tablet.

1. Reload the record.
2. Compare it with your entry.
3. Enter again only what is still correct, and save.

Where the drafts menu shows "Not saved:" and a reason, read the reason. The system does not show this in every form. After any refused save, check the record on the screen. The system never saves your entry again by itself.

## 8. Switch user

In bench mode the top bar shows **Switch user**. If you have unsaved entries on the tablet, the system asks before it signs you out. Save the entries you need, discard the others, and then confirm.

## 9. Add to the home screen and updates

- You can add MicroLIMS to the home screen of the tablet from the browser's install option.
- When a new version is ready, the screen shows "A new version is available." with a **Reload** button. The message does not appear while an entry window is open. Press **Reload** only when you have finished your entry.

## 10. What this draft does not cover

- Bench mode sizes were not checked on a real tablet, and not with gloves, for this draft.
- Your laboratory's rules for tablets (who may use them, how they are locked down, how often they are cleaned) are set in the approved SOP, not in this guide.
