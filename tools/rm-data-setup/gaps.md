## A. Still open after the decisions

1. **Karl Fischer water cannot carry MoistureRole Water as decided.** The backend allows the Water role only on a Karl Fischer titration test whose titrant has a mg water/mL titre (`TestDefinitionMasterDataService.ValidatePerContainerAndMoistureRole`, `TitrationDefinitionRules`). The STMs give no KF reagent, titre or endpoint (051 STM:42; 063 and 064 STM:38). Loaded instead: `RM-KF` as a plain Measurement test (analyst enters water %), without the role, so the water rows (051 2.5 %, 063 and 064 5 %) are judged but feed no correction. The anhydrous-basis HPLC assays of 051, 063 and 064 are held back anyway; they cannot be released until the lab gives the KF reagent, titre, endpoint and replicates and `RM-KF` becomes a KF titration test. [RM-KF]
2. **ICP rows for % w/w results** (025 Total Se 40.9-42.6 %, Se as selenate NLT 99.0 %, Se as selenite NMT 1.0 %; 150 USP Content of sodium 54.0-59.8 %, Potassium NMT 0.5 %). ICP spec rules take only mineral rows with a label claim or ug/g impurity rows. The change waits for the ICP methods (all held back). [RM-ICP-SE, RM-ICP-NA, RM-ICP-K]
3. **025 Sodium Selenate, which result is the reported total Se** (one AAS procedure, selenate result x 78.9/188.94, no speciation step for selenate/selenite). Needed to release the held-back ICP method. (STM:36; spec:17-19)
4. **051 Sildenafil, assay injections.** Which reference solutions the assay injects and which SST applies (STM:43). Needed to release the held-back HPLC assay method. [RM-HPLC-SILD-AS]
5. **063 / 064 paraben assays, moisture in 100/(100-MC).** The formula is copied from another STM; MC is the working-standard moisture while the limit is on the anhydrous substance. Also needs A1. (063 STM:39; 064 STM:39) [RM-HPLC-PPB-AS, RM-HPLC-MPB-AS]
6. **HPLC/ICP method details** the lab must supply (run times, standard injections, correlation, element view, flask volumes, RSD limits): per method in section B and `reports/held-back.md`.
